Where they live

RepositoryWhat it is
MyFeeds-AI__Investor_RelationsThe investor pitch, its source, and the documents behind it. Publishes investor.myfeeds.ai.
myfeeds-aiThe engine: the pipeline that produced the posts recovered here — RSS ingestion, entity extraction, graph building, persona selection, output.

Both are public and open source. investor.myfeeds.ai is published from the first; the second is the pipeline that generated every persona briefing in the recovered archive.

The exception to indexing rather than copying is anything that exists only in the Internet Archive. That has no owner left to defer to, so this repository holds it — see the archive, where every item records the capture it came from.
