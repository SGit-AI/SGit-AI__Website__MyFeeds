Every sentence on this site about what a specific product does or does not export is
currently marked `unverified`, because it was written from memory rather than from a
primary source. That marker is honest; it is not a resting place.

For each reader this site names: find the vendor's own export documentation, record the
URL and the date checked, list the fields the export carries, and move the sentence to
sourced — or correct it.

**Done when** no `unverified` marker remains on `/read-state/` without a dated reason
beside it.
